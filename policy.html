Privacy Policy

School ERP

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

VTCodeX ("we", "us", "our") operates the School ERP application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, protected, and handled when students, parents, teachers, school administrators, or other authorized users use our School ERP application.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the School ERP is configured and used by the respective school, the App may process the following categories of information:

Account and Login Information

  • Username or student ID
  • Password or authentication credentials
  • User role, such as student, teacher, parent, or administrator

Student Information

  • Student name
  • Class, section, and academic information
  • Attendance information
  • Behaviour records and feedback
  • Academic progress and related information

Fee Information

  • Fee records
  • Payment status
  • Due dates
  • Other school-related fee information

Other School Information

  • Syllabus and study material
  • Complaints or requests submitted through the App
  • Teacher or school remarks
  • Information provided by authorized school personnel

We only process information that is necessary for providing the School ERP services and features.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through the App may be used for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate authorized users
  • To provide access to school-related information
  • To display attendance records
  • To display fee and payment status
  • To provide academic and behavioural information
  • To provide syllabus and study materials
  • To manage complaints, feedback, and requests
  • To maintain and improve the App
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the service
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements

4. School and Authorized Users

School-related information may be entered, managed, or updated by authorized school administrators, teachers, or other authorized personnel.

The information displayed to a student or other user depends on the permissions and access provided by the respective school.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be accessed or processed by authorized school personnel and service providers where necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or provide the School ERP service.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the School ERP service, maintain school records, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Retention periods may also depend on the requirements and policies of the respective school.

8. Account Deletion and Data Deletion

If you want to request deletion of your School ERP account or associated personal information, you may contact our support team.

Account Deletion Request

Please send an account deletion request from your registered email address and include your registered username or student ID.

Email: support@vtcodex.com

After verifying the request, eligible personal information will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate, subject to applicable legal, security, accounting, academic, or record-retention requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

The School ERP may be used by students, including students who are minors, under the supervision and authorization of their school, parents, or guardians, as applicable.

Student information is processed for legitimate educational and school-management purposes and is generally provided or managed by authorized school personnel.

If a parent or guardian believes that information relating to a child has been provided or processed improperly, they may contact us using the contact information provided below.

10. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party infrastructure, hosting, security, analytics, notification, or other service providers where required to operate the service.

Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and subject to applicable agreements and policies.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App or associated web services may use cookies, local storage, tokens, or similar technologies where necessary for authentication, security, preferences, or functionality.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us.
